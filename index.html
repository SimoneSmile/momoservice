Helpful answers

Before you book.

Still unsure? Call or text and we will help you decide the best next step.

Is the $100 service call applied to the repair?+

Yes. When you approve the repair, the scheduled service-call charge is credited toward the repair.

Do you repair every brand?+

We service all major appliance brands. Share the brand and model number in your text when possible so we can prepare for the visit.

How does the 90-day warranty work?+

Completed repairs include a 90-day warranty. Contact us if the same repaired issue returns during that period.

What should I send when requesting an appointment?+

Please include your name, service address, appliance type, a short description of the issue, and whether you need a regular or same-day visit.
